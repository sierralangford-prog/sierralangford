# Simplify the portfolio and stop repeated photos

## Recommended structure

- Reduce the main menu to **Home, Work, Experience, Contact**.
- Keep About, Testimonials, and Strengths available through concise homepage sections and the footer instead of separate top-level tabs.
- Move selected work directly below the homepage introduction so recruiters see it immediately.

## Homepage

- Shorten the introduction to one clear positioning statement and two actions: Work and Contact.
- Show a small, curated selection of projects near the top.
- Keep one short testimonial preview with a link to the full testimonials page.
- Remove the large photo gallery and other repeated or overly long sections.

## Photo rules

- Audit every image used across the homepage, project cards, project pages, Experience, About, Testimonials, and Strengths.
- Assign each real photo to one best location only; remove cover/gallery repetition within the same project too.
- If a project has no unused, relevant image, show it cleanly without a photo and ask Sierra for one rather than borrowing another image.

## Site-wide simplification

- Trim repeated introductions, extra buttons, duplicate image galleries, and secondary calls to action.
- Keep project example links prominent and related projects as title-only links.
- Preserve every unique project, testimonial, credential, and supplied example.

## Verification

- Confirm the reduced menu works on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm no image URL is rendered in more than one portfolio location.
- Check the homepage and representative project pages for readability, broken links, and visual overload.
